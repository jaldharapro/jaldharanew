INSTALLATION SERVICES

We undertake planning and implementation of all kinds of rainwater harvesting systems ranging from economy to premium and micro to macro level. The projects are undertaken on turnkey basis or as part of the work depending on customer requirements and other factors. Project execution is done keeping in mind high standards of work such as:

  • Good quality workmanship
  • Best quality materials with right dimensions
  • Adherence to relevant standards
  • Detailed & transparent costing
  • Timely completion of project



DUAL SCREEN FILTER


Simple design, High Functionality

  • Innovative first of a kind design
  • Perfect alternative to conventional desilting pit
  • Compact design ensures installation in very less space
  • Can be installed underground, above ground or partially above ground
  • Easy & quick to install even by a normal labour
  • High flow rate due to shearing action of water on SS304 V-Wire screen makes rainwater harvesting possible even from large roofs
  • Dual SS304 Sieve bend V-Wire screens with unique non-clogging, continuous slot design for sustained and better screening
  • In-built inlet & vent pipe connections to reduce on-site plumbing work
  • Can be provided with overflow connection
  • Can be provided with first flush option to further improve its efficiency
  • Filtered rainwater can be led to groundwater recharge system or storage tank for reuse
  • Easy, infrequent and low cost maintenance-Can be conveniently done even by user
